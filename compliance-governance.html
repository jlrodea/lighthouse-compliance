Services / Compliance programs

Rules that protect the business, and that people actually follow.

Compliance simply means having clear rules and controls for your biggest risks, and being able to show they work. We help you build that, sized to your company.

Best for:

  • Companies building their first program
  • Clients or contracts asking for proof
  • Policies that exist only on paper

How a program project runs

Three steps

  1. Assess

    We learn your business, review what you already have and spot the gaps.

  2. Design

    We build or fix the code of conduct, policies, controls and reporting procedures your risks call for.

  3. Implement

    A prioritized plan with owners, documentation, training and metrics to track progress.

How we can help

Services

Program design & review

Starting from zero, or not sure what you have still fits? We build it or fix it.

You get: a gap assessment, risk map, policies and a prioritized improvement plan.

What's included
  • Understand your business, structure and key relationships
  • Review existing documentation and identify gaps
  • Define program components within the agreed scope
  • Responsibilities and escalation paths
  • Incident reporting and handling procedures
  • Training and communication
  • Recommended controls and monitoring
  • Implementation plan with owners and metrics

Code of conduct & policies

Documents written for your company that tell employees exactly what to do when things get tricky.

You get: a code of conduct and the specific policies your risks require, without contradictions.

Typical documents
  • Code of conduct
  • Conflicts of interest
  • Gifts, hospitality and business courtesies
  • Anti-corruption, where relevant
  • Whistleblowing and escalation
  • Record retention
  • Third-party review procedures
  • Confidentiality and information handling

Risk assessment & control matrix

A one-page view of your biggest risks: what already protects you, who's responsible and what's missing.

You get: a prioritized view of your risks and a clear list of fixes with owners.

What's included
  • Identify relevant processes
  • Describe risk scenarios, causes and consequences
  • Examine existing controls
  • Identify gaps and weaknesses
  • Documented rating criteria
  • Mitigation measures, owners and priorities

Procedures & training

Turn policies into everyday steps: who does what, when, and how it gets recorded.

You get: workflows, checklists, forms and training your team can realistically follow.

Can include
  • Operating procedures and workflows
  • Responsibility matrices
  • Checklists, forms and records
  • Training materials
  • Guides for supervisors and process owners
  • Exception escalation
  • Metrics to check procedures are applied

Ethical culture check

Good policies fail when targets push people the other way, or nobody trusts the reporting line.

You get: recommendations to improve communication, training, reporting and consistency.

What we examine
  • How ethical expectations are communicated
  • Whether policies make sense to the people applying them
  • Whether owners know their obligations
  • How exceptions are handled
  • Barriers to reporting problems
  • Whether training fits real situations

Every program is tailored to your company and the legal requirements that apply to it. We don't sell one-size-fits-all templates, and no policy can guarantee compliance on its own.
