What to include in your message

You don't need to know the technical terms. A short description is enough:

  • Your name, company and role
  • Your preferred language
  • The area you need help with (see below)
  • A brief description of the problem
  • Roughly when you need support

Areas we help with

  • Handbooks & workplace policies
  • Workplace conflict
  • Turnover & onboarding
  • Compliance programs
  • Supplier & partner vetting
  • Sanctions & export red flags
  • Acquisitions
  • Incident reviews
  • Something else

Please don't send confidential information yet. Avoid sharing sensitive employee records or documents related to a possible investigation in your first message. If we work together, we'll set up an appropriate, secure way to exchange that information.
