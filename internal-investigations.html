How it works

A structured review in four steps

  1. Scope

    Agree on the questions to answer and the limits of the review.

  2. Gather

    Review authorized documents and records, and hold structured interviews when appropriate.

  3. Document

    Build a timeline, record findings, inconsistencies and open questions.

  4. Fix

    Recommend controls and procedures to prevent it happening again.

Sensitive cases need extra care with confidentiality, records, conflicts of interest and legal safeguards. Scope always depends on the case, applicable law and professional credentials, and some situations call for an attorney or specialized investigator directly.

Facing an incident?
