How we can help

Pick the problem you're facing

Employee handbooks & workplace policies

Get your rules out of old emails and people's heads, and into one clear handbook everyone can use.

You get: a review of what you have, a revised handbook or specific policies, and a rollout plan.

What's included
  • Develop or review employee handbooks
  • Document internal policies and procedures
  • Attendance, punctuality and absence policies
  • Clarify responsibilities and job expectations
  • Communication and escalation procedures
  • Use of company resources and tools
  • New-hire onboarding procedures
  • Consistency check across existing policies

Harassment & misconduct prevention

Employees know where to report. Supervisors know what to do. Leadership knows when to step in.

You get: clear reporting procedures, defined responsibilities and training for staff and supervisors.

What's included
  • Review of existing policies
  • Reporting and escalation procedures
  • Defined internal responsibilities
  • Guidelines for receiving and documenting reports
  • Training materials for employees and supervisors
  • Review of reporting channels
  • Clearer, more accessible procedures
  • Anti-retaliation measures

Workplace conflict

Stop putting out the same fire. We find what keeps the conflict going (unclear roles, workload, leadership or communication) and fix that.

You get: an analysis of the causes and concrete recommendations to resolve and prevent it.

What's included
  • Structured information gathering
  • Who and which processes are involved
  • Review of related policies and responsibilities
  • Organizational factors behind the conflict
  • Recommendations on communication, coordination and roles
  • Procedures for similar situations in the future
  • Escalation protocols
  • Guidance for team leaders

Team effectiveness

Delays, errors and duplicated work usually come from how the work is organized, not from people not trying.

You get: a map of priority issues and an improvement plan with owners and follow-up.

What we look at
  • Clarity of roles and responsibilities
  • Task and workload distribution
  • Communication between departments
  • Bottlenecks and wait points
  • Duplicated tasks or missing owners
  • Quality of work procedures
  • Onboarding and training
  • Supervision and feedback

Hiring, onboarding & retention

When new hires leave fast, the cause is often what they were told, how they were onboarded or how they're supervised.

You get: a structured onboarding process, first-weeks checklists and metrics to track retention.

What's included
  • Hiring process review
  • Clear job competencies and expectations
  • Review of what candidates are told
  • Structured onboarding design
  • Checklists for the first days and weeks
  • Initial training materials
  • Organizational causes of turnover
  • Onboarding and retention metrics

Organizational assessments & workshops

Find out what's really getting in the way of communication, teamwork and buy-in, then fix it.

You get: a scoped assessment, practical recommendations and workshops for supervisors and teams.

What's included
  • Organizational assessments with a defined scope
  • Structured interviews, when appropriate
  • Communication and coordination review
  • Training needs analysis
  • Workshops for supervisors and teams
  • Support for organizational change
  • Tools to clarify roles and expectations
  • Internal feedback mechanisms

Our difference

Why psychology makes the difference

A policy only works if people understand it, trust it and can follow it. Our founder's background in organizational psychology means we look at motivation, leadership and team dynamics, not just paperwork. It isn't therapy, and it isn't about blaming individuals: it's about fixing how people, roles and processes fit together.

Policies are reviewed with the applicable law in mind. Legal questions, formal investigations of sensitive complaints and disciplinary decisions are referred to a licensed attorney or specialist when needed.
