No surprises in how we work, either.

Every engagement follows the same five steps, so you always know what's happening, what you'll get and what it covers.

  1. Understand the problem

    You tell us what's going on and what you want to achieve. We listen and ask the right questions.

  2. Agree on scope

    A clear proposal: what we'll review, what information we need, what you'll receive and its limits.

  3. Analyze

    We review your documents, processes and the people factors behind the problem.

  4. Report findings

    What we found, why it matters, what's still unknown and what to do next, in order of priority.

  5. Put it into practice

    If you want, we help turn recommendations into policies, procedures, training or action plans. Decisions always stay yours.

Different problems, same method.

High turnover, and a complaints line nobody uses

  • We review workplace policies and onboarding
  • We find where communication breaks down
  • We check whether reporting feels safe and simple
  • Result: clearer roles, updated procedures and supervisor training

A new international distributor

  • We review the counterparty's corporate information
  • We flag inconsistencies that need checking
  • We assess the risk of the relationship
  • Result: an informed decision, and a rule for when future partners need sign-off
