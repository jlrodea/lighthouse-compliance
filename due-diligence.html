How we can help

Services

Supplier & partner checks

A background check on a new or existing supplier, distributor, intermediary or partner.

You get: a written report on the company, its owners, red flags and open questions.

What's included
  • Identify the legal entity and its stated activity
  • Review corporate information and documents
  • Public records and legitimate available sources
  • Documentary inconsistencies
  • Relevant adverse information
  • Red flags, missing information and recommended next checks

Ownership & beneficial owners

Untangle multi-layered structures across entities and countries to see who's really in control.

You get: an ownership picture, where information allows, and what still needs verifying.

What's included
  • Available ownership structures
  • Relationships between entities
  • Corporate documents provided
  • Beneficial owners, where possible
  • Inconsistencies between statements and documents

Enhanced checks for higher risk

When something looks off or the stakes are high, we dig deeper, focused on what matters.

You get: a focused deeper review and clear recommendations for your decision.

What's included
  • Factors that justify an enhanced review
  • Additional documentation
  • Complex corporate structures
  • Adverse information analysis
  • Documented open questions

Sanctions & export red flags

Selling or sourcing internationally? We flag counterparties and transactions that need a closer look.

You get: documented findings, possible matches to verify, and when to escalate to a specialist.

What's included
  • Initial risk assessment information
  • Counterparty and relationship review
  • Possible matches or red flags to verify
  • Transaction documentation
  • Missing information, findings and limitations

Before an acquisition

Find out what compliance problems come with the company you're buying.

You get: a report on policies, governance, controls and gaps, plus areas that need deeper review.

What's included
  • Policies and procedures
  • Governance structure
  • Third-party processes
  • Documentation gaps
  • Known incidents and controls

After an acquisition

Bring the acquired company's policies and controls in line with yours.

You get: a gap assessment, priority controls and an integration plan.

What's included
  • Compliance gap assessment
  • Review of inherited policies
  • Priority controls
  • Responsibilities and processes
  • Improvement plan and procedures
